Protecting Wild Birds

Wild birds are vital to ecosystems, supporting pollination, seed dispersal, and pest control. However, habitat loss, pollution, and climate change threaten their survival.

How You Can Help

  • Support conservation organizations with donations or volunteering.
  • Create bird-friendly spaces with native plants and clean water sources.
  • Reduce pesticide use to protect birds and their food sources.
  • Advocate for policies that preserve natural habitats.
